# Privacy Policy

## 1. The short version

- Your video and audio files are processed entirely on your device. They are never uploaded, copied to our servers, or read by us.
- We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location, and we do not collect them.
- There is no account, no sign-in, no subscription, and no in-app purchase.
- The app contains **one analytics SDK (Umeng)** that sends anonymous device and usage statistics over the network, so we can see crash rates and how many people use the app. That is the only data that leaves your device, and it never includes your media files or file names.
- We do not sell your data, and the app shows no advertising.

## 2. Data processed on your device only

### 2.1 The files you choose

The app converts video to audio and converts between audio formats (MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, FLAC, OGG).

- You pick the source file yourself through the Android system file picker (Storage Access Framework) or the system photo picker. The app requests no permission to scan your media library.
- The file is decoded and re-encoded locally with codecs bundled in the app (MediaCodec, LAME for MP3, Vorbis for OGG).
- The finished file is written to a folder on your device (by default `Music/AudioPick`), or to a folder you choose.
- Decoding, encoding, and file writing all happen on the device. **No part of your media content is transmitted, stored remotely, or accessible to us.**

### 2.2 Conversion history

The app keeps a local history list so you can find and replay your results. Each entry stores: output file name, output format, file size, duration, source kind (video extraction or format conversion), the on-device location of the output file, the bitrate, and the date and time.

- This history lives only in a database inside the app's private storage on your device. It is not sent to us.
- You can delete single entries, select several, or clear the whole list in the History screen. Deleting an entry also deletes the output file it points to.

### 2.3 Preferences

Settings you choose are stored locally: default output format, default quality, save location, auto-play after completion, continue in background, keep temporary files, bitrate, sample rate, and channel count. Nothing is sent to us.

## 3. Data sent by our analytics provider

We use **Umeng+ Mobile Analytics** (`umeng-common` 9.7.4.1 and the `umeng-asms` / ZID identifier component 1.8.7, provided by Umeng Tongxin (Beijing) Technology Co., Ltd., an Alibaba group company) to understand crashes and basic usage.

The SDK may transmit:

| Category | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Device identifiers | An app-install identifier and a persistent device identifier generated by the SDK (ZID), built from Android system identifiers and device characteristics. Not linked to your real identity by us. |
| Device & system info | Model, manufacturer, brand, OS version, screen size and density, language, CPU architecture, memory class |
| Network info | Carrier name, connection type, IP address (used by the provider to infer approximate region/country — the app itself never asks for location permission) |
| App info & performance | App package name and version, channel, launch and session counts and duration, foreground/background events, crash stacks and error logs |

What this means in practice:

- **Purpose:** aggregate statistics and crash diagnosis only. We do not build advertising profiles, do not retarget, and do not sell or share this data with advertisers or data brokers.
- **No content:** file names, file paths, the contents of your media, your history, and your preferences are excluded from analytics payloads.
- **No consent gate:** the analytics SDK starts on the app's first launch. If you do not want any analytics data sent, uninstalling the app stops it, and you can ask us to help by contacting the address above. We can look up an installation by its SDK identifier if you provide it, but we cannot link an identifier to a named person because we never collect names.
- The provider's own privacy policy, which governs how they handle the data they receive, is at [https://www.umeng.com/policy.html](https://www.umeng.com/policy.html). Their processing may take place in China or another jurisdiction, which matters if you live in the EEA or the UK — see section 8.

## 4. Permissions we request, and why

| Permission | Why | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`, `ACCESS_WIFI_STATE` | Let the analytics SDK reach its servers and report the connection type. Core conversion works fine offline. | Always declared |
| `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | Show progress for a conversion that continues in the background, and a "task completed" notification. | Asked only if you start background processing on Android 13+ |
| `FOREGROUND_SERVICE`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PROCESSING` | Keep a long conversion alive when you leave the app or lock the screen. | Automatic, only while a task runs |
| `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` (`maxSdkVersion` 28) | Write the output into the shared `Music` folder on Android 8–9 only. | Asked on Android 8–9 only. On Android 10+ the app does not request it; it writes via the system media store or a folder you grant |

The app requests **no** permission for camera, microphone, location (precise or coarse), contacts, calendar, call log, phone, SMS, or media scanning.

## 5. Other ways data moves

- **Sharing:** when you tap Share, the output file is handed to an app you pick (messaging, cloud drive, email) through a short-lived, single-file grant. What that app then does with the file is governed by its own privacy policy, not ours.
- **System backup:** the app allows Android's automatic backup, so your history database and preferences may be included in your device's encrypted cloud backup or in a device-to-device transfer. This is stored and managed by Google under Google's terms, not by us, and you can turn off "Back up to Google Drive" in system settings. Uninstalling the app removes its data from that backup on the next backup cycle.
- **Play Store links:** the Settings screen can open the Play Store listing ("Rate this app"). The Play Store handles any data involved under Google's own privacy policy.
- **Feedback email:** tapping Feedback opens your own email app with a pre-filled address. The app does not send mail by itself and never reads your mailbox or contacts.
- **No servers of our own:** the app talks to no API or backend that we operate. There is nothing for us to breach on our side.

## 10. Security

There is no account system and no upload of your content, so the main risk surface is the device itself. We mitigate it by design:

- Media access is limited to files you explicitly pick, via the system picker's short-lived or revocable grants.
- The app stores history and preferences in its private sandbox, readable only by the app (no root, no world-writable files).
- Output files are only handed to other apps when you press Share.
- Release builds are minified and obfuscated, and the bundled native codecs are compiled into the app rather than fetched at runtime.
- No TLS traffic exists other than the analytics SDK's HTTPS reporting, which we do not control.

No app can guarantee absolute security. If you believe you found a vulnerability in this app, email noww6446@gmail.com and we will investigate.

## Appendix B — Open-source components

Conversion quality depends on third-party code bundled inside the APK: MediaCodec (Android platform), LAME (MP3 encoding, LGPL), and libvorbis / Tremor-style Ogg Vorbis encoding. These are computation libraries; they run on your device and send nothing anywhere. Jetpack Compose, Room, DataStore and Navigation are AndroidX libraries that likewise do not transmit data on their own.
